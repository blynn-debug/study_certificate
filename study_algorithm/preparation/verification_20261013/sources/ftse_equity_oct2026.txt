FTSE Classification of Equity Markets –  
FTSE Equity Country Classification 
September 2026 Annual Announcement 
Published: 06 October 2026 
A formal annual review of country classification within the FTSE global equity indices is conducted every September 
using a comprehensive, transparent and consistent methodology. This annual review incorporates ongoing equity 
country classification research and classifies markets as Developed, Advanced Emerging, Secondary Emerging or 
Frontier within the FTSE global equity indices. 
Please refer to the FTSE Equity Country Classification Process document for further details. 
The results of the 2026 Annual FTSE Equity Country Classification Review can be found in the remainder of this 
announcement.

1. FTSE Equity Country Classification Watch List 
 
To provide investors with visibility of the potential movement of markets between classifications, FTSE Russell 
maintains a Watch List of markets that are under review and being monitored for reclassification. 
 
The following market was a member of the Watch List following the March 2026 Equity Country Classification Interim 
Review: 
 
• 
Egypt: possible reclassification from Secondary Emerging to Frontier. 
 
 
2. Progress of Watch List Markets since March 2026 
 
 
Egypt – Reclassification from Secondary Emerging to Frontier market status 
 
Egypt was added to the Watch List from September 2025 for possible reclassification from Secondary 
Emerging to Frontier market status. This was due to Egypt experiencing a steady decrease in the number of eligible 
Egyptian index constituents within FTSE Russell global benchmarks. This resulted in the market failing to meet the 
minimum securities count requirement as of year-end 2024 and month-end June 2025, with only one Egyptian index 
constituent in the FTSE Emerging Index (Large and Mid Cap), with the minimum requirement being at least two eligible 
index constituents.  
 
Consequently, Egypt was added to the Watch List for potential demotion from Secondary Emerging to Frontier market 
status. Although the market continued to fail to meet the minimum securities count requirement based on data as of 
year-end 2025, the government’s economic reforms and initiatives introduced by the Egyptian Exchange resulted in 
increased liquidity in the Egyptian equity market. This resulted in an additional Egyptian security meeting the index 
eligibility for the FTSE Emerging Index for the March 2026 index review and also the September 2026 index review. 
This resulted in three Egyptian Mid Cap designated index constituents and three Small Cap designated index 
constituents meeting the minimum size and securities count requirement. 
 
Therefore, as Egypt meets the nine FTSE Quality of Markets criteria required for retaining Secondary Emerging market 
status within the FTSE Equity Country Classification scheme and the minimum size and securities count requirement, 
and following the feedback received from the FTSE Equity Country Classification Advisory Committee and the FTSE 
Russell Policy Advisory Board, the FTSE Russell Index Governance Board confirms, and is pleased to announce, that 
Egypt will be removed from the Watch List and retained as a Secondary Emerging market within FTSE Russell equity 
indices.  
   
For further details on the minimum investable market capitalisation and securities count thresholds please refer to the 
FTSE Equity Country Classification process guide.

3. Previously confirmed reclassification 
 
Greece – reclassification from Advanced Emerging to Developed market status 
 
As part of the FTSE Equity Country Classification September 2025 annual review, FTSE Russell announced the 
reclassification of Greece from Advanced Emerging to Developed market status, effective from the open on Monday 21 
September 2026. 
 
Further details on the implementation of Greece to Developed market status is available in an FAQ published on the 
FTSE Russell website: Reclassification of Greece FAQ 
 
 
Vietnam – reclassification from Frontier to Secondary Emerging market status 
 
As part of the FTSE Equity Country Classification March 2026 interim review, FTSE Russell confirmed the 
reclassification of Vietnam from Frontier to Secondary Emerging market status, commencing with an initial 10% 
implementation from the open on Monday 21 September 2026, followed by three further tranches in March 2027, June 
2027 and September 2027. 
 
Further details on the implementation of Vietnam to Secondary Emerging market status is available in an FAQ 
published on the FTSE Russell website: Reclassification of Vietnam FAQ 
 
 
Nigeria – reclassification from Unclassified to Frontier market status 
 
On 27 August 2026, FTSE Russell confirmed the reclassification of Nigeria from Unclassified to Frontier market status, 
effective from the open on Monday 21 September 2026.

4. Other Markets 
 
Indonesia (Secondary Emerging market status)  
 
FTSE Russell continues to closely monitor capital market integrity reforms in Indonesia. Following the postponement of 
certain index changes for Indonesia at the March, June and September 2026 index reviews, FTSE Russell is assessing 
the effectiveness of recently implemented reform measures in supporting an orderly and stable transition back to 
standard index review processes, as outlined in the FTSE Russell FAQ on the Treatment of Indonesian Securities. 
 
Indonesia has implemented a broad range of market reforms, including enhanced shareholder disclosure, expanded 
investor classification categories, minimum free float requirements, and enhanced market surveillance tools. These 
measures are intended to address previously identified concerns relating to data transparency and replicability. FTSE 
Russell will continue to monitor implementation and effectiveness of these measures and engage with market 
participants to gather feedback. 
 
FTSE Russell will provide an update on the treatment of Indonesian securities ahead of the December 2026 index 
review, taking into consideration the progress of market reforms and feedback received from stakeholders. 
 
Indonesia’s Secondary Emerging market status remains unchanged. FTSE Russell is not placing Indonesia on the 
Watch List and will continue monitoring market developments and engaging with market participants as part of its 
ongoing assessment. 
 
 
South Korea (Developed market status) 
 
Settlement – Funding Requirement 
 
FTSE Russell continues to monitor settlement funding requirements in the South Korean market. The South Korean 
market authorities are implementing a range of reforms aimed at enhancing settlement liquidity and reducing funding 
pressures associated with Settlement Progress Payments (SPPs), including the use of securities as collateral, the 
alignment of exchange and institutional settlement processes, and measures to facilitate KRW funding. While these 
initiatives are intended to improve settlement efficiency and reduce liquidity requirements for international investors, 
implementation remains ongoing and market participants have indicated that further operational experience is required 
to assess their effectiveness. FTSE Russell will continue to monitor developments and engage with market participants 
on this issue. 
 
Investment Alert / Investment Risk Lists 
 
FTSE Russell continues to monitor the operation of the Korea Exchange (KRX) Market Warning System, including 
Investment Alert and Investment Risk designations. In response to feedback from market participants regarding the 
potential impact of these measures on index replication, FTSE Russell has updated the FTSE Global Equity Index 
Series Ground Rules to provide greater clarity regarding the eligibility and treatment of securities subject to market 
warning measures. FTSE Russell will continue to engage with market participants and monitor developments in this 
area.  
 
FTSE Russell will continue to monitor developments in South Korea’s short selling, settlement, and market-warning 
framework, and welcomes feedback from market participants, as well as continued engagement with the South Korean 
market authorities to address the issues outlined above. 
 
 
Taiwan (Advanced Emerging market status)  
 
FTSE Russell notes that the Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan) (CBC) maintains a daily net New Taiwan 
Dollars (TWD) purchase limit of US$100 million for Foreign Institutional Investors (FINIs) to support the orderly 
functioning and stability of the foreign exchange market. This limit can impact the ability of index trackers to replicate 
benchmark treatment, such as significant corporate events and index reviews. FTSE Russell continues to engage with 
the Taiwanese market authorities on this issue.

Türkiye (Advanced Emerging market status) 
 
Following the postponement of certain index changes for Türkiye at the September 2026 index review, FTSE Russell 
continues to engage with Borsa İstanbul and the Capital Markets Board of Türkiye (CMB) which is reviewing the 
feasibility to improve the granularity underpinning the Central Securities Depositary’s (MKK) published free float 
figures. 
 
FTSE Russell is not placing Türkiye on the Watch List and its Advanced Emerging market status remains unchanged. 
 
 
5. New Addition to the Watch List 
 
 
Oman (Frontier market status)  
 
Oman is added to the Watch List for possible reclassification from Frontier to Secondary Emerging market status. 
Oman meets the nine FTSE Quality of Markets criteria required for attaining Secondary Emerging market status within 
the FTSE Equity Country Classification scheme, and meets the minimum investable market capitalisation and 
securities count requirement for a Secondary Emerging market, based on data as of month-end June 2026. 
 
The minimum securities count requirement requires at least three securities meeting the index eligibility requirements 
for Large or Mid Cap and a total of at least five securities meeting the index eligibility requirements for Large, Mid and 
Small Cap.  For further details on the minimum investable market capitalisation and securities count thresholds please 
refer to the FTSE Equity Country Classification Process guide. 
 
FTSE Russell will conduct another minimum size and securities count requirement assessment on Oman based on 
data as of year-end December 2026 and provide an update on the Watch List status of Oman as part of the FTSE 
Equity Country Classification March 2027 Interim Review. Watch List indices reflecting the reclassification of Oman as 
a Secondary Emerging market will be published by the beginning of 2027.

ABOUT FTSE RUSSELL 
FTSE Russell is a leading global provider of index and benchmark solutions, spanning diverse asset classes and investment 
objectives. As a trusted investment partner we help investors make better-informed investment decisions, manage risk, and seize 
opportunities. 
Market participants look to us for our expertise in developing and managing global index solutions across asset classes. Asset 
owners, asset managers, ETF providers and investment banks choose FTSE Russell solutions to benchmark their investment 
performance and create investment funds, ETFs, structured products, and index-based derivatives. Our clients use our solutions for 
asset allocation, investment strategy analysis and risk management, and value us for our robust governance process and 
operational integrity. 
For over 35 years we have been at the forefront of driving change for the investor, always innovating to shape the next generation of 
benchmarks and investment solutions that open up new opportunities for the global investment community.
